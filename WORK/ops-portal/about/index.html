Profile

I work at the intersection of renewable energy, analytics engineering, and interactive dashboards. Day to day I use Python, R/Shiny, SQL, and modern data platforms — shipping tools people can click, not slide decks that go stale overnight.

Before Germany I built experience across engineering, R&D education programmes, university teaching, and educational technology in Lebanon and Saudi Arabia. I entered Germany on 31 August 2022 (EU Blue Card) and have worked as a Data Engineer since then — first at Entrenco GmbH (Regensburg), now at SMA Solar (Kassel).

I also run a public dashboard hub on this VPS — the same stack I use to ship energy analytics, live demos, and stakeholder-ready apps.
